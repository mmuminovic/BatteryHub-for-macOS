# Privacy Policy — Battery Hub

**Effective date:** November 25, 2026

Battery Hub ("the app") is a menu bar utility for macOS, developed by Muhamed Muminovic ("we"). It shows the battery level of your connected Bluetooth devices. In short: **the app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.**

## Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not use any third-party services. The app does not connect to the internet.

## Bluetooth

The app asks for Bluetooth permission so it can read the names and battery levels of the Bluetooth devices that are already connected to your Mac (for example headphones, a mouse, a keyboard or a trackpad). This information is only shown in your menu bar and menu. It is never stored, sent anywhere or shared. The app does not scan for, pair with or control other devices.

## Information stored on your Mac

The app saves only the position of its menu bar item, using standard macOS settings inside its sandboxed app container. Nothing else is saved.

## Launch at login

This is optional and off by default. It uses the standard macOS login item service, and you can turn it off at any time from the app's menu or in System Settings → General → Login Items.

## Diagnostics from Apple

If you have chosen in macOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash reports and usage statistics through App Store Connect. These do not identify you. They are governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), and you can turn them off in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements**.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
